## Method (code/198_l8_f2_conditional_mvmr.py → code/output/genetics/L8_F2_conditional_mvmr.json)
- Shared-instrument MVMR infeasible: the 31 total-body BMD instruments and the 193 HSM lead variants are **disjoint (zero overlap)**. So condition *within the HSM-instrument frame*: use each HSM lead variant as instrument, look up GEFOS total-body BMD beta/SE on the same SNPs.
- 59 of 193 HSM lead variants carried a usable GEFOS BMD effect (per-mode n = 3–9 after subsetting).
- IVW-MVMR (Sanderson–Windmeijer 2015) for θ_BMD|HSM and θ_HSM|BMD per mode × outcome; Egger-MVMR intercept = horizontal pleiotropy; Sanderson–Smith conditional F = instrument strength for the BMD coefficient. No new external munge; only outcome SEs used for weighting.

## Key results (honest grading)
- **Knee OA**: θ_BMD|HSM positive in 4/5 estimable modes (HSM2 +0.133, HSM4 +0.283, HSM8 +1.065 p=0.0013, HSM10 +0.539 p=3.4e-6); HSM1 negative but ns (p=0.55). Best-powered modes (HSM8/HSM10) clearly positive+significant → conditioning on hip geometry leaves the BMD→knee-OA effect intact → **geometry is not the transmission path**. This formally corroborates the single-variable F2 null.
- **Hip OA**: θ_BMD|HSM heterogeneous and sign-flipping (e.g. HSM6 marginal +0.19 → conditional −0.92; HSM1/HSM6 neg, HSM2/4/10 pos) → inconclusive; consistent with the generally weaker hip-OA evidence.
- **Instability**: conditional F 0.11–11.87 (only HSM10 ~11.9; most <1), per-mode n 4–9, I² 48–98%, several Egger intercepts sig (e.g. HSM1/HSM2 hip p<1e-7). HSM instruments are strong for geometry but weak for the added BMD coefficient → estimates are **direction-indicative, not magnitude-precise**; no cross-mode pooled coefficient quoted.
- θ_HSM|BMD significant in several modes but direction-heterogeneous → no hip-shape axis reproducibly drives OA (matches single-variable finding).
