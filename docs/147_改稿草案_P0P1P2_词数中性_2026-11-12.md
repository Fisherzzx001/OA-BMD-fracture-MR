## P0（必改，4 处）

### P0-1 · K 尺度重建"循环"（R2-1 / 逻辑断点 B）
- **位置**：L57 Effect-scale reconstruction。
- **问题**：作者自承 `The Z ratio is unity by construction, a same-source check that cannot identify K`；审稿人读作"主效应全靠不可识别常数"。**但实测（G2/S17-a）**：K 由沉积 log-odds SE 反推 N_eff 识别，非由 Z 比；β/SE 同乘常数 → 所有 p/Q/I²/Egger/power 对 K 不变，仅绝对 OR 随 K 变（±10% → OR 变 <0.5%）。批评"循环"过重、"影响"被夸大。
- **改稿**：
  - old: `The Z ratio is unity by construction, a same-source check that cannot identify K.`
  - new: `The Z ratio is unity by construction and cannot identify K; the log-odds SE does (K-sensitivity in the supplement; p-values are K-invariant).`
  - old: `Two verified scale corrections were applied.` → new: `Two scale corrections were applied.`（腾 1 词）
- **数值不变**：完全不写具体 K/因子/区间数字，只指向已存在的 S17-a；数字字面量零新增。

### P0-2 · areal BMD ≡ 成骨设定点概念置换（R1-1 / 断点 A）
- **位置**：L93。
- **问题**：areal BMD 被同时当作"临床强度代理"(L35)、"质量/尺寸/几何复合量"(L81)、"单一设定点读数"(L93)。且双膦酸盐论证方向反了——抗吸收也升 areal BMD 却降膝置换，恰证明 areal BMD **不能**区分形成/吸收，削弱而非支持"areal BMD=成骨设定点"。
- **改稿**：
  - old: `A systemic, polygenic bone-forming set-point readable as areal BMD would protect at the whole-bone endpoint and raise OA risk at the joint — the Systemic Osteogenic Set-point (SOS) ... [39]) and from the broader osteoporosis–osteoarthritis 'bone–cartilage paradox' [3, 4].`
  - new: `We distinguish an observed from a mechanistic claim: areal BMD shows opposite endpoint signs (established here), whereas one systemic polygenic osteogenic set-point (SOS; Figs. S21, S23) is the untested hypothesis proposed to generate them — distinct in kind from the bone-forming triad (osteophytes, enthesophytes, high bone mass; OR 3.00, 95% CI 1.96–4.58 [39]) and the 'bone–cartilage paradox' [3, 4].`
  - old: `anti-resorptives also raise areal BMD, yet bisphosphonate users had a lower knee-replacement rate (RR 0.71, 0.66–0.77) [40], implicating formation rather than density.`
  - new: `anti-resorptives also raise areal BMD yet bisphosphonate users had lower knee-replacement (RR 0.71, 0.66–0.77) [40], so areal BMD cannot by itself distinguish formation from resorption.`
- **数值不变**：OR 3.00/CI 1.96–4.58/RR 0.71/CI 0.66–0.77/引用 [39][40][3,4] 全部原样保留（数字字面量匹配已验证）；仅逻辑主语从"implicating formation"改为"cannot distinguish formation from resorption"。

### P0-3 · 五/十部位外推 + F2 措辞过强（R2-2 / R2-4）
- **位置**：L101 Conclusion（原 F2 措辞），并需同步 L93 `geometry does not absorb the signal`。
