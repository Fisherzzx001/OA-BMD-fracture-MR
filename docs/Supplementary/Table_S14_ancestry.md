# Supplementary Table S14. Population-ancestry composition of the 13 GWAS

The BMD exposure instruments are European-only, whereas the knee-OA outcome is 87.31% European (multi-ancestry meta-analysis); LDSC uses a European-only reference, so cross-population LD differences between the BMD instruments and the multi-ancestry knee-OA outcome can bias r_g and the MR estimates. This ancestry mismatch is a primary limitation (§4). The OA_knee percentages are taken from the GWAS Catalog entry and the originating Nature report of the multi-ancestry meta-analysis.

**MRlap overlap correction** (full per-pair values in the manuscript's Table S14 note). No overlap proportion was supplied; MRlap v0.0.3.3 ran on its default and does not return a scalar overlap fraction — overlap is accommodated through the internally estimated cross-trait LDSC intercept λ (`int_crosstrait` in `code/output/mrlap_overlap_results.csv`), which ranged from −0.0361 to +0.0153 (|λ| ≤ 0.037) across the ten pairs. The UKB overlap counts tabulated above are reported independently and were not fed to MRlap; all ten corrected estimates retained their direction (median +20.5% away from the null), so no conclusion depends on the default.

**Burgess overlap-bias bounds (bias = overlap fraction × r_g; §2 of the manuscript).** The tabulated counts are BMD 31,873 participants (100% UKB), OA_selfrep 462,933 (100% UKB), fracture 22,330 UKB participants (8.4% of 264,973; 70% of BMD), and OA_knee UKB participation not separately reported (overlap upper bound 31,873 / 1,208,397 = 2.6%). Hence BMD→OA_knee bias ≤ ~0.3–0.7% (2.6% × r_g 0.12–0.25), BMD→OA_selfrep ~0.8–1.7% (6.9% × 0.12–0.25) and BMD→fracture ~3.4–4.5% on the 8.4%-of-fracture basis, ~11.8–12.4% on the symmetric geometric-mean overlap √(0.701×0.084)=24.3%, and ~34–36% on the literal 70%-of-BMD basis; all act in the direction of r_g. The manuscript's S14 previously wrote '~12% on the 70%-of-BMD basis', which mislabeled the geometric-mean figure as the exposure-referenced one; the three denominators and their correctly derived biases are now reported side by side (Table S33 Part A). Because Burgess's exact form is olsbias × overlap.prop × (1/F), the overlap × r_g expression omits the 1/F attenuation (aggregate F≈44–74 here) and is a conservative ceiling: even ~34–36% is a worst-case bound, not an expected bias. MRlap's scale-free relative displacement (+15.3% total-body, +19.4% femoral-neck) transfers onto the Table-2 IVW as OR 1.475 and 1.521, narrowing the log-odds gap to GEFOS 1.55 from 23.1%/19.9% to 11.4%/4.4% (Table S33 Part B); under 70% sharing of the BMD sample the positive control is not a strictly two-sample comparison, and MRlap moves it toward, not away from, GEFOS. MRlap retained all ten directions (median +20.5% away from the null), so the positive BMD→knee-OA effect survives the worst-case overlap correction.
