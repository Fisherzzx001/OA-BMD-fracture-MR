Sensitivity: across the full grid (n_bmi ∈ {100,300,800}) the θ_BMD direction is stable at every site; at n_bmi=800 the BMI instruments over-absorb BMD signal and Fc(BMD) falls to 5.6, so n_bmi=100 is the primary configuration. Directional pleiotropy: the MVMR-Egger intercept is null for total-body, femoral-neck, arms and legs (p=0.27–0.41) and nominal for lumbar-spine (p=0.027); radial-trimmed estimates are direction-concordant with IVW at all six tabulated rows. A femoral-neck→fracture control row (not tabulated) gave θ_BMI=−0.004 (p=0.93) with a non-null Egger intercept (p=2.1×10⁻⁴), mirroring the femoral-neck instrument's known pleiotropy signal in the univariable analysis. The BMI GWAS shares UK Biobank participants with the BMD exposure and the self-reported OA outcome, so this conditioning analysis is read alongside the overlap bound (≤2.6% for BMD→OA) reported in §2.
