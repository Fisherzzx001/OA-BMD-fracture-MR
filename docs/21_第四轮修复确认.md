## P0 — Zhou 2025 方向矛盾被当作"延伸"宣扬（必改 1）

**问题：** 第 26 行称本文正向 BMD→knee-OA "extends" Zhou 2025 的 femoral-neck BMD→hip-OA 发现；但同句承认 Zhou 报的是 *negative* 因果效应。联网核实 Zhou 2025（*Front Genet* 16:1597005）原摘要：β=−2.17, P<0.01，即更高 BMD **降低** hip OA 风险（负向）；而本文 BMD→knee OA 为**正向**（+0.017~+0.028，全 p<1e-4，power 0.93–1.00）。方向相反却称"延伸"，逻辑与文献双重不自洽。

**修复：**
- **引言（§1，line 26）**：将 "Our BMD→knee-OA analysis **extends** that femoral-neck BMD→hip-OA finding…" 改为 "Our BMD→knee-OA analysis, by contrast, finds a positive effect (higher BMD raising knee-OA risk) that **contrasts with** Zhou's negative femoral-neck estimate; the hip-versus-knee direction reversal is discussed in §4.2."
- **讨论（§4.2，line 158）**：新增显式讨论句 —— "Zhou et al. [5] reported that higher femoral-neck BMD *protected* against hip OA (a negative MR effect), whereas our ten-site analysis shows higher BMD *raises* knee-OA risk (positive). This reversal is a hip-versus-knee contrast rather than a contradiction of the literature — hip OA is a localised disease of a heavily weight-bearing joint whose subchondral bone responds to load differently from the knee — and it cautions against generalising any single BMD→OA estimate across joints."

**核验：** "extends that femoral-neck" 已不存在；"contrasts with Zhou" + "hip-versus-knee direction reversal is discussed in §4.2" 已落地。

## P1 — §4.2 机制段无据断言（必改 2）

**问题：** "the WNT16 and SOX6 loci regulate both systemic BMD and subchondral osteoblast activity, and GDF5 … could mediate a profile that raises BMD while promoting osteophyte and subchondral-sclerosis phenotypes that manifest as OA" 三处亚软骨骨机制断言无任何文献支撑。

**修复（§4.2，line 158）：** 将整段机制断言降级为明确**假设**：
- "offered as a hypothesis rather than a demonstrated pathway"；
- 三基因确实为 BMD 与 OA 共有位点 —— 用本文自有 lead instrument SNPs（rs3779381/WNT16、rs7108738/SOX6、rs224333/GDF5，见 §2.2）佐证其"候选共享效应子"身份，属已建立的性状关联，非新断言；
- 明确 "direct evidence that they mediate a profile raising BMD while promoting osteophyte and subchondral-sclerosis phenotypes that manifest as OA is, however, lacking and requires joint-specific functional validation"，删除原 "subchondral osteoblast activity" 无据具体机制措辞。

**核验：** "subchondral osteoblast activity" 已不存在；"offered as a hypothesis rather than a demonstrated pathway" 已落地。

## P1 — §4.3 系统性 vs 局部机制先强推后自撤（必改 3）

**问题：** 原句 "arguing against a purely local mechanical-load mechanism and being more consistent with a systemic bone-mass mechanism… although the 0.005 difference between sites is within sampling error and is suggestive rather than proof" —— 先推出"系统性机制"再自撤，0.005 在抽样误差内，不构成结论。

**修复（§4.3，line 162）：** 形式化为假设并声明未正式检验：
- "This pattern is more consistent with a systemic bone-mass mechanism than with a purely site-specific mechanical-load effect, **but it is a hypothesis rather than a demonstrated finding**: we **did not formally test the weight-bearing-versus-non-weight-bearing difference**, the 0.005 gap is within sampling error, and the reconstructed β scale widens the credible intervals, so the systemic interpretation remains suggestive."

**核验：** "arguing against a purely local mechanical-load mechanism" 已不存在；"we did not formally test the weight-bearing-versus-non-weight-bearing difference" 已落地。
