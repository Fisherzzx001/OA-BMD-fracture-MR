### Ethics approval and consent to participate / 伦理

Not applicable. This study is a secondary analysis of publicly available, fully anonymised genome-wide association study (GWAS) summary statistics. No new human participant data were collected, contacted, or identifiable. The source GWAS (UK Biobank, Genetics of Osteoarthritis consortium, GEFOS consortium) each obtained appropriate ethics approval and participant consent, as reported in their original publications.

### Data availability / 数据可用性

All genome-wide summary statistics analysed in this study are publicly available:

- **Knee OA (radiographic):** Genetics of Osteoarthritis (GO) Consortium 2025, *Nature* 641:1217–1224 (PMID 40205036); summary statistics via the GWAS Catalog / GO consortium release.
- **Self-reported OA:** IEU OpenGWAS dataset `ukb-b-14486` (N=462,933), https://gwas.mrcieu.ac.uk.
- **BMD (10 DXA sites):** Qian Y, et al. *Genomics Proteomics Bioinformatics* 2025 (PMID 41206123); GWAS Catalog accessions GCST90568448–GCST90568458 (per-site N 30,449–32,017, European UK Biobank), https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas.
- **Any-fracture:** GEFOS consortium, Trajanoska K, et al. *BMJ* 2018;362:k3225 (PMID 30158200), N=264,973.

### Code availability / 代码可用性

★ The complete analysis pipeline (LD score regression; strict PLINK-equivalent clumping at r²<0.001 over 10 Mb; two-sample MR with IVW, MR-Egger, weighted median, Cochran's Q, radial outlier removal and leave-one-out; the MR-PRESSO global distortion test; sample-scale N reconstruction and correction; and all figure-generation scripts, written in Python 3) is available from the corresponding authors on request and will be deposited in a public GitHub repository with a Zenodo DOI upon publication.
